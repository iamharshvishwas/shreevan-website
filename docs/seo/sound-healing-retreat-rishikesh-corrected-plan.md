## 1. Final Verdict on Gemini Plan

Gemini's plan is directionally useful, but it should not be implemented as-is.

The plan correctly moves the page toward a stronger local retreat intent with `Sound Healing Retreat in Rishikesh`, adds AEO-style direct answers, adds comparison content, and suggests better schema alignment. However, some keyword volumes are outdated or not aligned with the supplied CSV, and several phrases are too strong for a wellness page because they can imply clinical or guaranteed healing outcomes.

Use Gemini's structure as a base, but use this corrected version for implementation.

## 5. Corrected Page Structure

### Hero

Eyebrow:

`Sound-Supported Rest Modality - Shreevan Wellness Rishikesh`

H1:

`Sound Healing Retreat in Rishikesh`

H2 / tagline:

`Sound Bath, Tibetan Singing Bowls and Gentle Sensory Rest`

Hero lede:

Use simple, clear language. The copy should explain that the session is guided, acoustic, calm, and optional. It should not promise healing, nervous-system repair, trauma release, better sleep, or medical benefit.

Responsible boundary:

Keep this visible near the hero:

`Sound sessions are optional wellness practices. They are not medical treatment, trauma therapy, guaranteed nervous-system repair, or a replacement for professional clinical care.`

### Quick Answer

H2:

`What is sound healing and how does a sound bath work?`

Direct answer should explain:
- Sound healing as a guided listening and rest practice
- Sound bath as a session where guests rest while listening to acoustic instruments
- Tibetan singing bowls, chimes, bells, silence, and pauses
- Retreat use case: slowing down, sensory rest, transition from active practice to quiet time

Avoid:
- Claims of cellular healing
- Cure language
- Strong medical claims
- Guaranteed emotional release

### Educational Guide

Recommended H2:

`What to understand before booking a sound healing retreat`

Sections:

1. `What sound healing means at Shreevan`
2. `Why people explore sound bath sessions`
3. `How a sound bath works inside a retreat`
4. `Sound bath vs meditation vs music listening`
5. `How Shreevan keeps sound therapy grounded`
6. `What beginners should know`
7. `When to be careful`

The comparison section is important for AEO/GEO because it gives AI systems a clear extractable table.

### Daily Rhythm Section

H2:

`How sound healing fits into the daily retreat rhythm`

Stages:

1. Comfort setup
2. Guided sound bath
3. Slow return
4. Reflection and integration

Use this section to connect sound healing with the broader retreat experience, not as a standalone medical or spiritual promise.

### Suitability Section

H2:

Columns:

- May suit you if

This section is essential for trust, responsible wellness positioning, and SXO.

## 6. Corrected Claim Safety Rules

Allowed language:

- may support rest
- can feel calming for some guests
- helps create a quiet listening environment
- supports transition between active practice and rest
- gives the mind a gentle sound anchor
- can be easier than silent sitting for some beginners

Avoid language:

- proven healing
- cures anxiety
- repairs the nervous system
- releases trauma
- heals cells
- balances chakras as a guaranteed outcome
- treats tinnitus
- fixes sleep disorders
- removes emotional blocks

`sound therapy` can be used, but the page must clarify that Shreevan offers a wellness practice, not clinical therapy.
