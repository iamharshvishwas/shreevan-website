# Guided Meditation Page - Image Briefs

Page: `/modalities/guided-meditation`  
Purpose: Image generation direction for Google Flow or any visual generation tool.  
Goal: Create images that support SEO, AEO, GEO, AIO and SXO without looking medical, fake, over-spiritual or stock-like.

## Overall Visual Direction

All images should feel like Shreevan Wellness:

- Calm, premium and grounded.
- Natural Rishikesh retreat setting.
- Soft morning or evening light.
- Simple cotton clothing.
- Wooden floor, cushions, shawls, journals and plants.
- No forced smiles.
- No dramatic spiritual effects.
- No hospital, clinic or therapy-room feeling.
- No text, logo or watermark in the image.
- Photorealistic, 16:9 landscape, web-safe composition.

The page is about a guided meditation retreat in Rishikesh. The images must show supported stillness, attention practice and daily rhythm. They should not claim medical healing or miracle transformation.

## Image 1 - Hero Image

Recommended filename:

`guided-meditation-retreat-rishikesh-hero.webp`

Placement:

Hero section, right side of the H1 and intro copy.

Content meaning:

This image should immediately tell visitors that this is a guided meditation retreat in Rishikesh. It should show calm facilitation, beginner-friendly practice and a retreat environment. The visitor should feel that the practice is held, guided and safe, not strict or intimidating.

What should be in the image:

- One facilitator guiding one or two adult guests.
- Seated meditation on cushions or simple mats.
- Natural Rishikesh environment visible through a window or open side.
- Soft morning light.
- Relaxed posture, eyes closed or soft gaze.
- Calm, simple retreat hall or open-air space.
- Neutral cotton clothes in white, beige, sage, cream or soft earth tones.
- Enough empty space for web cropping.

What should not be in the image:

- Large crowded class.
- Religious icon as the main subject.
- Dramatic aura, glowing light or fantasy effect.
- Medical equipment.
- Therapy couch.
- Extreme yoga posture.
- Artificial stock-photo smiles.
- Text, logo or watermark.

SEO / accessibility alt text:

`Guided meditation retreat in Rishikesh with facilitator-led seated practice`

Caption:

`A calm practice setting helps beginners enter stillness without pressure.`

Prompt base:

`Photorealistic premium wellness retreat in Rishikesh, a calm facilitator gently guiding one or two adult guests through seated guided meditation in a bright retreat hall, soft morning light, simple cushions, natural greenery outside, warm wooden floor, cotton clothing, beginner-friendly atmosphere, peaceful but grounded, no text, no logo, no watermark, 16:9 landscape.`

## Image 3 - Daily Rhythm / Reflection Image

Recommended filename:

`meditation-reflection-daily-rhythm-rishikesh.webp`

Placement:

Daily rhythm section near:

`How this practice is held in the daily rhythm`

Content meaning:

This image should show what happens after meditation. The page is not only about sitting practice. It is about carrying stillness into daily rhythm. The image should show journaling, integration and quiet self-observation.

What should be in the image:

- One retreat guest journaling after guided meditation.
- Guest seated near a window, terrace or open retreat space.
- Notebook visible.
- Cushions or folded mat nearby.
- Soft white, cream or earthy clothing.
- Natural light.
- Calm, reflective expression.
- Rishikesh retreat atmosphere, possibly greenery or river-side hills in background.

What should not be in the image:

- Person looking like they are posing for a commercial.
- Overly dramatic sadness or intense emotion.
- Laptop, phone or office items.
- Medical setting.
- Group class.
- Text, logo or watermark.

SEO / accessibility alt text:

`Guest journaling after guided meditation at a Rishikesh retreat`

Caption:

`Reflection helps guests carry the practice into daily life.`

Prompt base:

`Photorealistic retreat guest journaling quietly after guided meditation near a window or open terrace in Rishikesh, simple cotton clothing, notebook in hand, cushions nearby, soft morning light, calm reflective mood, warm wooden floor, natural greenery outside, premium grounded wellness retreat aesthetic, no forced smile, no text, no logo, no watermark, 16:9 landscape.`

## Master Style Prompt

Use this before every image-specific prompt:

`Photorealistic premium wellness retreat in Rishikesh, calm natural light, warm wooden floor, soft cotton clothing, simple cushions, quiet open-air or riverside retreat setting, grounded Indian wellness aesthetic, peaceful but realistic, natural colors, no exaggerated spirituality, no medical setting, no stock-photo smile, no text in image, no logos, no watermark, 16:9 landscape.`

## Negative Prompt

Use this with every image:

`No text, no watermark, no logo, no glowing aura, no fantasy effects, no hospital, no clinic, no therapist couch, no crowded class, no extreme yoga posture, no religious icon as the main subject, no dark moody lighting, no smartphone, no laptop, no office setting.`

## Image Selection Checklist

Before using any generated image, check:

1. Does it clearly support guided meditation, not yoga therapy or sound healing?
2. Does it feel like a Rishikesh retreat environment?
3. Does it avoid medical or therapy claims?
4. Is there enough clean composition for desktop and mobile cropping?
5. Are hands, faces and body posture realistic?
6. Is there no text, logo or watermark?
7. Does it look premium but simple?
8. Does it match Shreevan's calm visual tone?

## Final Recommended Set

If only two images are generated first:

1. Hero image.
2. Daily rhythm / reflection image.

If three images are generated:

1. Hero image.
2. Practice clarity image.
3. Daily rhythm / reflection image.

Three images are better because they support the user journey: first impression, method clarity and integration.
