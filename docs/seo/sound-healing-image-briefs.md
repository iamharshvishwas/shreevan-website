# Sound Healing Page - Image Briefs

Page: `/modalities/sound-healing`

Purpose: Image generation direction for Google Flow or any visual generation tool.

Goal: Create images that support SEO, AEO, GEO, AIO and SXO without looking medical, fake, over-spiritual or stock-like.

## Overall Visual Direction

All images should feel like Shreevan Wellness:

- Calm, premium and grounded.
- Natural Rishikesh retreat setting.
- Soft morning or evening light.
- Simple cotton clothing.
- Wooden floor, cushions, bolsters, blankets, plants and natural textures.
- Real acoustic instruments such as Tibetan singing bowls, chimes, bells, mallets or sound bowls.
- No forced smiles.
- No dramatic spiritual effects.
- No clinical therapy-room feeling.
- No text, logo or watermark in the image.
- Photorealistic, 16:9 landscape, web-safe composition.

The page is about a sound healing retreat in Rishikesh. The images must show guided acoustic rest, sound bath setup and quiet integration. They should not claim medical healing, frequency cures or miracle transformation.

## Image 1 - Hero Image

Recommended filename:

`sound-healing-retreat-rishikesh-hero.webp`

Placement:

Hero section, right side of the H1 and intro copy.

Content meaning:

This image should immediately show that the page is about a guided sound healing retreat in Rishikesh. It should show a calm facilitator, a small guest setting and real acoustic instruments. The visitor should understand the session without reading the whole page.

What should be in the image:

- One facilitator guiding a sound bath session.
- One or two adult guests resting or sitting comfortably.
- Tibetan singing bowls or sound bowls clearly visible.
- Optional chimes, bells or mallets near the facilitator.
- Natural Rishikesh environment visible through a window, open hall or terrace.
- Soft morning or evening light.
- Warm wooden floor, mats, bolsters and folded blankets.
- Neutral cotton clothing in white, cream, beige, sage or soft earth tones.
- Enough clean space for desktop and mobile cropping.

What should not be in the image:

- Large crowded class.
- Concert-like performance setup.
- Microphones, speakers or stage lighting.
- Glowing aura, fantasy energy waves or mystical visual effects.
- Hospital, clinic or medical equipment.
- Therapy couch.
- Artificial stock-photo smiles.
- Text, logo or watermark.

## Negative Prompt

Use this with every image:

`No text, no watermark, no logo, no glowing aura, no fantasy energy waves, no hospital, no clinic, no therapist couch, no concert stage, no microphone, no speaker system, no crowded class, no extreme yoga posture, no religious icon as the main subject, no dark moody lighting, no smartphone, no laptop, no office setting.`

## Image Selection Checklist

Before using any generated image, check:

1. Does it clearly support sound healing, not yoga therapy or guided meditation?
2. Are Tibetan singing bowls or sound bath instruments visible where needed?
3. Does it feel like a Rishikesh retreat environment?
4. Does it avoid medical or guaranteed-healing claims?
5. Is there enough clean composition for desktop and mobile cropping?
6. Are hands, faces and body posture realistic?
7. Is there no text, logo or watermark?
8. Does it look premium but simple?
9. Does it match Shreevan's calm visual tone?

## Final Recommended Set
