## Educational Guide Structure

### Section 1: Understanding chakras as symbolic maps

Use this section to explain the seven-chakra language without presenting it as a proven medical model.

Correct framing:

`In yogic traditions, chakra language is often used as a symbolic map for awareness. At Shreevan, these themes are used for reflection, journaling and practice inquiry. The goal is not to prove or diagnose a chakra state. The goal is to help guests notice patterns in daily life with more honesty and steadiness.`

Theme examples:

- Root: stability, routine, safety and grounding.
- Sacral: creativity, adaptability and relationship with change.
- Solar plexus: discipline, boundaries and personal direction.
- Heart: compassion, grief, forgiveness and connection.
- Throat: voice, expression and truthfulness.
- Third eye: clarity, observation and discernment.
- Crown: meaning, humility, stillness and connection.

Avoid these phrases:

- `digestive vitality`
- `emotional release`
- `reduce mental overwhelm`
- `open blocked energy`
- `clear trauma`
- `activate higher consciousness`

Use safer alternatives:

- `traditional association with discipline and digestion`
- `reflect on emotional patterns`
- `support steadier attention`
- `explore symbolic themes`
- `notice what feels unresolved`
- `reflect on meaning and stillness`

### Section 2: Grounded yogic inquiry vs commercial mystical claims

| Core method | Breath, meditation, journaling and reflective inquiry | Psychic scans or instant energetic clearing |

### Section 3: How chakra practice fits into a retreat

Recommended 4-step rhythm:

1. Theme setting: facilitator introduces one symbolic theme in plain language.
2. Embodied settling: breath, simple movement or meditation helps the guest slow down.
3. Guided reflection: journaling prompts connect the theme to daily life.
4. Integration: guests connect the insight to food rhythm, rest, communication, boundaries or post-retreat routine.

### Section 4: Suitability and safety

H2:

`Who this may suit, and when to slow down`

May suit you if:

- You are spiritually curious and prefer grounded language.
- You want to explore values, voice, purpose, compassion or transition.

Be careful if:

- You want someone to diagnose your life through chakra language.

Consult a professional if:
