## Placement Decision

Use exactly 3 images for this page:

1. Hero: consultation and readiness.
2. Practice clarity: sattvic routine and comparison.
3. Retreat experience: rest and integration.

Do not add images to every section. Too many images will make the modality pages feel repetitive and heavy. Three images are enough for SXO, image SEO and trust while keeping the page clean.
